# Feature discussion

## Do
- Give a direct opinion with reasons. Push back when a feature does not fit.
- Plan first when the user asks for a plan or a discussion. Build only after agreement.
- Offer the simpler general approach. Example: let the sender review uploaded ID documents, instead of country-specific ID checks. It works in every country.
- Ask who asked for it. With no users or traffic, skip demand-side features such as abuse limits or account holds.
- Add a few related ideas that clearly belong.

## Do not
- Do not build a feature the user parked until someone asks for it.
- Do not offer billing work while the payment account is under verification.
- Do not make legal claims beyond what the product supports.
