
# Type and brand references

## How to use
1. Start from the brand facts: industry, tone, region, languages. A font must support every script the client needs (Devanagari, Arabic, Cyrillic, CJK). Check this first.
2. Fonts: see the font working on real sites (Fonts In Use, Max Best Of, Typewolf) before you pick. Pick one family for the UI and at most one for display.
3. If the client cannot pay for a font, find a free lookalike (Font Alternatives).
4. Colours: start from a mood (Pinterest), then make the palette (Coolors). Check contrast. Define light and dark tokens.
5. Logos: collect 5 to 10 references of the right style (wordmark, monogram, symbol) before you sketch.
6. Check every licence: web, app and logo use are often separate licences.

## Rules
- At most two font families on a site. One is often enough.
- Body text is at least 16 px on phones. Check the font at that size, not only in the hero.
- Self-host web fonts and subset them for speed.
- A logo must work in one colour, at 16 px (favicon) and on dark and light backgrounds.
- Do not trace or closely copy a reference logo.
